Implementation boundaries
The receiver keeps two QPSK training symbols and eight payload symbols per frame,
NFFT=1024, CP=64, active bins 20..52 and interpolation by 1024. Timing and channel
estimation use training, not known payload bits. Averaging aligns training phase
and leaves each tested frame out of its averaged channel estimate. It operates
within a stationary communication burst and buffers the burst; it is not claimed
as a real-time receiver for a moving channel. Original results are retained.

MATLAB column ordering, one-based saved indices, signed DAC rounding and MT19937
uniform-bit generation are implemented explicitly. Floating-point calculations
and resampling need not be bit-identical between the languages. The included
ics_export_python_reference.m and replay_matlab_reference() compare the SAME
samples and packet without instrument access. Bridge tests use synthetic MAT
files; they do not constitute a native MATLAB execution or lab-data comparison.

The position estimator is a placeholder. Five raw pilot waveforms are exported
as estimator inputs. No actual coordinates or positioning accuracy are claimed.
The working MATLAB source and release package have not been modified by this port.
