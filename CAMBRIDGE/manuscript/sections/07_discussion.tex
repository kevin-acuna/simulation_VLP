\subsection{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{subsec:discussion}
The accuracy of the system is limited by its models, not by noise (Section~\ref{subsec:res-bound}). With a single Lambertian order, the error cannot go below 3.14~cm on D2 (Section~\ref{subsec:res-budget}), and a tilted LED biases the estimates further (Section~\ref{subsec:res-leds}). A pattern model with an angle-dependent order and the tilt of the LED, calibrated on two planes so that the height of the LED also becomes identifiable, is the natural next step. The validation has its own limits. The experiments cover one room, one LED height and a volume of $1.2\times1.2\times0.36$~m. The response of the PD was taken from the literature and confirmed by the profile-consistency test, but not measured on the device.\del{ The ground truth relies on the gantry, on two tape measurements and on the CAD model of the receiver.} Reflections were not modeled, so that whatever they contribute is part of the model bias.

The rotation stage belongs to the laboratory setup. It served to study the method with controlled orientations, and its slow rotation sets the duration of an acquisition (Section~\ref{subsec:res-time}), which is therefore not a limitation of the method. In an implementation, the receiver would be reoriented by the platform that carries it, for instance a mobile robot that turns about its own axis or a drone that yaws while it hovers, or by a motor much faster than the stage. The duration of an acquisition is then governed by the rotation of that platform, since the signal requires only 10~ms per orientation (Section~\ref{subsec:res-window}). One tilted PD mounted close to the rotation axis suffices, with the normals given by an IMU or by the encoder of the motor. With an IMU, roll and pitch follow from gravity, whereas the heading must be referred to the room, as the stage zero was here. A residual heading error only rotates the estimate about the vertical through the LED, by 1.7~cm per degree at 1~m from it, without changing the distance or the height. Heading references for such platforms, acquisition during a continuous rotation, moving receivers and the use of several LEDs, whose directions can be intersected without any gain or pattern calibration, are left for future work.
