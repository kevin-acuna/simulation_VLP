# Cambridge TIM manuscript

- This folder is the manuscript, not the MATLAB laboratory or the independent digital-twin application. Preserve the user-supplied `IEEEtran.cls` and the original reference papers.
- `main.tex` uses the IEEE double-column journal format. The current authorized scope fills only Section II, `System and Measurement Model`, in `sections/02_system_measurement_model.tex`. All other sections/subsections, abstract and keywords are intentionally empty. Empty subsection headings in `main.tex` use `draftsubsection` to allow column breaks; replace that wrapper with ordinary `subsection` when adding their body text. Do not invent results, experiments, authorship, grant support, publication status or received dates.
- The present manuscript is a working draft. `AcunaTCOM` remains available in the bibliography as an unpublished local RV3 manuscript for the future introduction; confirm publication metadata before submission. Authors and affiliations in the new manuscript remain to be confirmed.
- Follow the root `rules_for_write_a_paper.md`: number displayed equations, introduce them with a colon, and do not insert a blank paragraph after an equation when the same paragraph continues. Reserve bold r for receiver position, not residuals.
- Geometry: one fixed LED, one fixed PD optical center over a scan, globally referenced arbitrary receiver normals, unknown x/y/z. Direction u points from PD to LED. Write cos(phi) and cos(psi_i) explicitly; never abbreviate them as c and s_i. Do not impose a common tilt, uniform azimuth grid or nonadaptive-scan assumption in Section II; special orientation patterns belong after the PEB analysis.
- Keep Section II concise and conventional: describe stationarity in prose, use received power P_t H_i plus noise and a Lambertian transmitter, and retain the general normalized receiver response R(psi). Do not reintroduce f_T, g(s), C_0 or lengthy metrological generalizations. Historical comparisons and novelty arguments belong in the introduction, which remains a skeleton until requested.
- The measurement chain uses optical-equivalent RSS in W after electrical calibration. Do not multiply an extra receiver cosine or responsivity into R(psi). A half-response angle is not a hard FOV cutoff. Cosine power and SQ are response specializations, not assumed device truth.
- Section II uses known actual normals as the baseline. Detailed pose-uncertainty, correlation, lever-arm and calibration-error models are deferred to the relevant later sections. When developed, distinguish auxiliary pose measurements from actuation jitter; a hold's pose offset is not averaged away by optical samples.
- Bibliographic anchors: Wang et al., TIM 2024, DOI 10.1109/TIM.2024.3383461 (tilted/azimuth-rotated PD, 2D likelihood, simulations and small-scale field experiments); Liu et al., Photonics Journal 2022, DOI 10.1109/JPHOT.2022.3163415 (rotating arm translates PD, fixed-height 2D, simulation-based ML reflection handling); local TCOM RV3 (transmitter steering and a separate cooperative K+1 ranging measurement).
- The geometry figure was removed from the manuscript at the user's request; the user will supply a replacement. Its previous assets/function are retained but are not included or generated by default. The active acquisition diagram is an original schematic, not measured hardware. `figures/generate_figures.py` uses installed NumPy and Matplotlib plus Times New Roman and exports the acquisition diagram as embedded-font vector PDF and PNG, with notation matching Section II.
