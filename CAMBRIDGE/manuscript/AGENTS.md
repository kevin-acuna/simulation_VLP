Write cos(phi) and cos(psi_i) explicitly; never abbreviate them as c and s_i. Do not impose a common tilt, uniform azimuth grid or nonadaptive-scan assumption in Section II; special orientation patterns belong after the PEB analysis.
- Keep Section II concise and conventional: describe stationarity in prose, use received power P_t H_i plus noise and a Lambertian transmitter, and retain the general normalized receiver response R(psi). Do not reintroduce f_T, g(s), C_0 or lengthy metrological generalizations. Historical comparisons and novelty arguments belong in the introduction, which remains a skeleton until requested.
- The measurement chain uses optical-equivalent RSS in W after electrical calibration. Do not multiply an extra receiver cosine or responsivity into R(psi). A half-response angle is not a hard FOV cutoff. Cosine power and SQ are response specializations, not assumed device truth.
- Section II uses known actual normals as the baseline. Detailed pose-uncertainty, correlation, lever-arm and calibration-error models are deferred to the relevant later sections. When developed, distinguish auxiliary pose measurements from actuation jitter; a hold's pose offset is not averaged away by optical samples.

## Observation-window experiment (user, 2026-10-29) - Results VI-K "Observation Window", fig_window.pdf
- Page count: do NOT trim the text to hold a page budget (user: up to ~15 pages is fine for now, they adjust later).
- MATLAB part 'window' (needs grid_results.mat + noise_results.mat; ~5 min): RSS of every D2 record recomputed on
  windows cut from the 100 ms waveforms (0.01-100 ms), up to 10 disjoint segments per record: 'spread' (starts
  evenly over the record = all phases of the 100 Hz flicker) and 'synced' (every 10 ms = one flicker phase);
  rectangular (main) and Hann. A rescaled per window by the coherent gain of the nominal-frequency projection
  (measured tone offsets -0.36 / -0.56 / +0.81 / +0.96 Hz -> 100 ms loss 0.21 / 0.52 / 1.07 / 1.49 %). WITHOUT
  this rescaling short windows look better (3.44 cm) only because their RSS is 0.5 % higher than the 100-ms
  calibration (vertical bias -1.6 -> -1.1 cm): an artefact, not a gain.
- Results (K=10 mean): 3.66 (100 ms) = 3.67 (10 ms), 3.71 (5), 3.93 (1), 4.89 (0.1), 9.46 cm (0.01 ms); K=5 3.74 /
  3.76 / 3.81 / 4.21. RSS spread: synced segments follow the off-tone noise floor * sqrt(100/T); spread segments
  ~2x higher between 0.1 and 10 ms (flicker-level-dependent amplitude, ~0.4 % rms at 500 kHz, jagged vs level ->
  8-bit digitizer, LSB 2.51 mV for all D2 records, tones 1.1-20.7 mV). Hann does not help (4.03 cm at 1 ms).

## Estimators (user, 2026-10-30) - Appendix B (sections/appendix_estimators.tex), Algorithm 1
- Bib key of the published TCOM paper: Acuna2026 (IEEE Trans. Commun. 74, 2026, DOI 10.1109/TCOMM.2026.3742967).
