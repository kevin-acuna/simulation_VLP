\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
% Commented for later review (user, 2026-11-02)
% \add{Single-LED visible light positioning (VLP) is attractive where only one luminaire is in view and for lightweight receivers such as drones, but a single received signal strength (RSS) cannot fix a three-dimensional (3D) position. This paper estimates the 3D position of a tilted photodiode (PD) reoriented to a few known orientations under one fixed LED: the shape of the RSS over the orientations gives the direction to the LED, and their common amplitude gives the distance through a calibrated LED model. A Fisher-information analysis for an arbitrary receiver response guides the choice of the tilt, and maximum-likelihood and ratio-based estimators are derived for a general response, including the SQ model of commercial PDs. On a room-scale testbed with the LED at 3~m, the mean 3D error is 3.66~cm over 125 positions at five heights and 4.10~cm over 131 blind random positions with five orientations. Nine calibration points, 10~ms of signal per orientation and a few milliseconds of computation per position suffice.}
\end{abstract}
