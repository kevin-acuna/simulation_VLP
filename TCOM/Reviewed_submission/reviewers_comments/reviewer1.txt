Comments to the Author
The authors present a single beam-steered LED single-PD OWP localization architecture, and derived the PEB. They discuss the selection of the orientation sets. They develop direction finding methods and demonstrate their feasibility by numerical simulation.

Some minor issues need to be addressed before acceptance.

L37-40, left, page 1: please correct the sentence “there is…, which …, such as …, are widely studied …”
L51, left, page 2: “and” should be added before “silicon-photonics”.

Table II: why do you use the z range of [0, 1.2] instead of a larger one such as [0, 2]? Which half-power semi-angle of the 3 values is selected in “optimization”?
Fig 2: how many random orientation sets are used?
Fig 3b: please clarify what you mean by “random configuration”. Is it one of the sets other than the optimized one? How do you select it?
Fig 4: I didn’t get why 60 deg half-power semi-angle has larger PEB. Doesn’t higher signal strength reduce PEB?
L49, left, page 10: correct the sentence “… apply the solves needed…”
